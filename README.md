# Pearls & Roses

AI consulting, agents, and Copilot for organizations that want AI to work in everyday operations.

> AI that reaches everyday work.

Pearls & Roses helps organizations introduce artificial intelligence in real business processes. We help teams choose the right solution, integrate it into existing workflows, and support implementation when AI should become part of daily work rather than remain a pilot project.

## Services

- AI consulting and advisory
- Solution selection and evaluation
- Integration support for existing systems and workflows
- Deployment of AI agents for practical business use cases
- AI readiness for websites and digital experiences

## Typical challenges we solve

Many organizations have already explored AI, but it has not truly reached everyday work. Common situations include:

- Copilot licenses have been purchased but adoption is still limited
- Teams are unsure which AI solution fits their processes
- AI projects stall because the right use case or integration is unclear
- Businesses need a practical and realistic path to automation

## How we work

2. We identify where AI can create value
3. We evaluate the right tools, integrations, and setup
4. We support implementation and adoption in day-to-day work
